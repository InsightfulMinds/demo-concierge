# The Demo Desk — Narrative

## The Problem

Companies that run product demos often focus nearly all of their energy on getting prospects into the demo experience. But the highest-leverage moment happens after interest is shown: a demo is completed, abandoned, revisited, paired with a form submission, or followed by a booked call. Too often, those signals are treated the same. Generic follow-up goes out, or nothing happens because the team does not want to spam the wrong people.

## The Insight

The problem is not the demo. The problem is the moment after. Intent signals are different: a booked call needs confirmation, an abandoned demo may need clarification, a repeat visitor may need a human, and a competitor or research-only visitor may need a polite close. A useful system respects those differences instead of treating every prospect as a drip-campaign recipient.

## The Solution

Demo Desk is an intent-driven follow-up operator that owns the critical minutes after a prospect shows interest. It evaluates the signal, classifies intent, and routes the next useful action: confirm, recover, nurture, escalate, or gracefully close. The system enforces Rule 0: no complete event data, no outreach. This discipline prevents low-quality follow-up and signals that lead quality matters more than volume. The back_to field routes every outcome to its next handler: null, escalate-human, or nurture-sequence. Outcomes are logged with confidence scores, so teams know which signals are reliable and which need human judgment.

Demo Desk is not a replacement for the sales team. It is a force multiplier that turns fresh intent into a crisp routing decision, giving humans the right conversations instead of another pile of guesses.
