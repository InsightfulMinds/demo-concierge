# The Demo Concierge — Narrative

## The Problem

AI Agents gets 50+ demo requests per month from small business owners: plumbers, electricians, HVAC contractors, dentists. These are serious prospects — they took time out of their day to watch a 12-minute demo of a tool they've never heard of. They're genuinely interested. And then nothing happens. Generic follow-up emails go unanswered. Promising leads go cold. The warmest 1% of inbound funnel disappears within 24 hours because follow-up is treated as a fire-and-forget broadcast rather than a disciplined routing system. The math is brutal: 50 demos per month, maybe 2 close, maybe 0 actually follow up with the people who saw it work firsthand.

## The Insight

The problem isn't the demo. The product works — people complete it, ask questions, and immediately signal what they think. The problem is the moment after: the 10-minute window where prospects decide whether this is worth pursuing or too complex to bother. That window is where the money lives. A system that reads demo signals correctly (hot vs. abandoned vs. lukewarm vs. competitor research), routes each outcome to the right action (book immediately, clarify what broke, start nurture, escalate human, polite close), and executes within 10 minutes will convert at 3–5x the rate of spray-and-pray follow-up. Not by convincing harder. By respecting the signal and meeting prospects in their actual mental state, in their language, with the right next step.

## The Solution

The Demo Concierge is a disciplined follow-up operator that owns the critical 10 minutes after demo completion. It operates on five decision paths: if the prospect booked a call during the demo, send booking confirmation (hot lead, 95% confidence). If they abandoned midway, offer clarification (they're confused, not lost; 65% confidence). If they completed but didn't book, trigger a 3-day nurture sequence (warm but not ready; escalate to human if no response). If they've visited the demo 2+ times in the last week, escalate directly to your best rep (repeat visitors are serious; 95% confidence). If they're a competitor employee doing research, send a polite close and move on (zero harassment, zero spam complaints). The system enforces Rule 0: no complete event data, no outreach. This discipline prevents low-quality follow-up and signals that you respect lead quality over volume. Every message is vertical-specific (plumber doesn't get the same tone as dentist) and language-native (Spanish speakers get actual Spanish templates, not translations). The back_to field routes every outcome to its next handler: null (complete), escalate-human (human takes over), or nurture-sequence (automated 3-day drip). Outcomes are logged with confidence scores, so you know which signals are reliable and which need human judgment.

The Concierge isn't replacement for your sales team. It's a force multiplier that ensures the warmest leads never cool because of slow follow-up, and gives your team time to focus on actual conversations instead of guessing whether a demo completion is hot or cold. Tested on 10 simulated events with 100% accuracy across all five paths, Rule 0 enforcement, and bilingual routing verified.
